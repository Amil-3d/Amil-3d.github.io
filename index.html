About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Virginia Tech. I got here the long way around — Lego, then RC cars, then drones, then printers — and somewhere in there taking things apart turned into designing them.

What I like most is the loop between a CAD model and a part you can hold. A design is a guess until it's printed, loaded, and flown, and the interesting work happens in what breaks and why.
