Project editor

Edit your projects here, hit Generate, copy the output, and paste it over projects.json on GitHub. Nothing here saves by itself — the copy-paste step is what publishes.

How to publish a change

  1. Add or edit projects below, then click Generate JSON and Copy.
  2. Go to github.com/Amil-3d/Amil-3d.github.io → click projects.json → pencil icon.
  3. Select all, paste, then Commit changes.
  4. For photos: open the img folder → Add file → Upload files → drag your photos in. Then list them in that project's Photos box, one per line, as img/folder/photo.jpg | what the photo shows.
  5. Site updates in about a minute.
Click Generate JSON.

Tips: Featured makes a card full-width with the photos beside the text — good for two or three of your strongest projects, not all of them. Card size set to Small gives a project a lighter card. The first photo in the list is the one people see first; the rest appear as thumbnails they can click through.
