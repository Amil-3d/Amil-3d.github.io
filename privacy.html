Privacy Policy

What Assistant is

Assistant is a personal tool that I, Amil Dhookie, built for my own use. I am its only user. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

Assistant connects only to my own Google account. It reads my Gmail and my Google Sheets so it can help me keep track of my own email and schedule. It does not access anyone else's Google account or data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to run Assistant for me. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with or transferred to anyone else.
